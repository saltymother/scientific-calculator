How calculations work here

This calculator does not use pre-baked trigonometric lookup tables or library wrappers. Every function is calculated from fundamental mathematical definitions:

  • sin(x), cos(x): Maclaurin/Taylor polynomial series with angle reduction modulo 2π.
  • tan(x): Fundamental quotient identity sin(x)/cos(x) with pole asymptote detection.
  • √x: Newton-Raphson quadratic root iterations: x_{k+1} = ½(x_k + S/x_k).
  • π & e: Machin's arctangent formula & Euler's sum of reciprocals of factorials.
